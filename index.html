What changed

Camera sway is off

It was the only thing that moved the picture over time, carrying every caption around the screen. It is no longer in "Spoof (Metaghost)" or "Spoof max (Metaghost)". On ten production sources tested before this change, Spoof without it still beat MetaGhost on all three numbers.

The zoom keeps the text

The zoom used to be aimed at whichever corner moved the clip furthest, cutting 5–8% off two edges, and with them top captions, subtitles and handles. Now the render first finds the clip's text (the same detector that keeps logos off captions) and only aims where every caption stays whole. The zoom amount stays as drawn unless the text leaves no room for it.

When text is everywhere

If no aim keeps all of it, the random crop goes first, then the zoom shrinks (75%, then 50%). Past that, the full zoom is aimed where it cuts the least text. Text already cut by the frame's edge in the source is not protected.

How to read the scores

TMK L1 and L2 lower is better

Meta's video fingerprint, comparing the whole clip. 1 means identical. Meta treats two videos as the same when both are 0.7 or more.

PDQ higher is better

Meta's image fingerprint, checked twice a second: how many of 256 bits differ from the source. Under 31 a frame counts as the same picture; about 128 is what unrelated pictures score.

Win

Reelforge is further from the source than MetaGhost on all three: lower L1, lower L2 and higher PDQ. Reelforge numbers better than MetaGhost's are green.

Worth knowing

  • Keeping the text costs distance: the median L2 moves from 0.588 to 0.647. It rises most on 1295, 1379, 1378, where the furthest corner was the one with the captions.
  • 1378 (L1 0.738 / L2 0.752): both TMK numbers at 0.7 or more, so Meta would still treat it as the source, though still further from it than MetaGhost. The current version had 0 such renders; on 1378 it stayed under 0.7 only by cutting the subtitles and sticker.
  • One render per source and tool, so small differences between sources are within noise.
